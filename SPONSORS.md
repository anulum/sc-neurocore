# Sponsors

This project is part of the **Neuromorphic computing** research group of independent open research by Miroslav Šotek (ANULUM, Switzerland).
Sponsorship through [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/anulum) pays for compute, hardware time and the hours to finish work properly. It does not buy results: findings are published whichever way they fall.

## How sponsors are acknowledged
- Sponsors at **$25/month and above** who choose this group are listed below.
- Sponsors at **$100/month and above** are also named in the README.
- Papers and datasets that a sponsor's support made possible say "supported by …". This is never a claim of endorsement or validation by the sponsor.
